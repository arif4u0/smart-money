Privacy Policy — Smart Money

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Smart Money is a personal automation tool used only by the owner of the Pinterest account @smartmoneypaycheck to publish that account's own original Pins (budgeting tips and product inspiration) to its own boards on a schedule.

What data we collect

We collect no data about any other person. The tool only uses:

  • The Pinterest account's own boards and Pins, through the official Pinterest API, with the account owner's permission (OAuth).
  • The Pin titles, descriptions and images the account owner creates for publication.

How data is used and stored

Account access tokens are stored only on the account owner's own system and are used solely to publish the owner's Pins. Tokens are never shared, sold, or sent to any third party. No passwords are stored. No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used on this page.

Third parties

Pins are published through Pinterest's official API and are subject to Pinterest's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
